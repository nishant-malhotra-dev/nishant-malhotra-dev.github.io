Real-Time Soft Sensor for Biopharmaceutical Fermentation using LSTM
Nishant Malhotra, Avinash Kumar — Applied ML, IIT Ropar (BM616)
Industrial AI  ·  Feb–Mar 2026
Trained a two-layer stacked LSTM on the IndPenSim v2 dataset (100 batches, ~113,900 rows, 2,239 raw features) to predict penicillin concentration, biomass, PAA, and viscosity from always-available online signals — addressing a 12-hour offline assay delay. Key design choices: masked MSE loss over sparse label positions only, strict batch-level splitting to prevent temporal leakage, PCA compression of 2,200 Raman channels to 10 PCs (99.9% variance retained), and feature-specific transforms (log1p, Yeo–Johnson, StandardScaler).
